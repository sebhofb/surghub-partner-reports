What is counted

  • Members are SURGhub learners who checked in with your organisation’s code. Membership is self-declared.
  • Learning counts from the day a member checked in: enrolments and certificates dated before that day are not included.
  • A member who checks out keeps what they did while checked in; nothing after they checked out is counted.
  • Check-in and check-out dates are the day SURGhub’s records first showed the change, so they can be a day or two late.
  • This page shows totals only. Its workbook adds one row per member and pathway course (when they enrolled, their progress, completion and certificate) under a code instead of a name: no names or emails. Groups smaller than 10 are not shown on their own on this page.
  • Global Surgery Foundation staff and test accounts are left out.
SURGhub, the UN Global Surgery Learning Hub, is a joint initiative of the Global Surgery Foundation (GSF) and UNITAR.
